Our Story

A quieter way to drink coffee

Ember & Oak began in 2014 with one small roaster, a short menu and a belief that coffee deserves a little ceremony. We buy directly from growers we have met, roast in small batches every week, and serve each cup on a warm vessel with a short note on what you are tasting.

The room is calm, the lights are low and nothing is rushed. Come for a morning espresso, stay for an afternoon pour over, or take the beans home.

We still roast on the machine we started with, name every batch by its date and share the numbers with the whole team. Pastries are baked before sunrise by a bakery two doors down, and every cup is poured by someone who trained with us for at least three months.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Delivery, allergens, loyalty and events. Cannot find your answer? Write to hello@emberandoakcoffee.com.

Yes. We deliver within 5 km of Oak Lane every day between 08:00 and 18:00. Choose Delivery at checkout and your order usually arrives in 30 to 45 minutes. Delivery is free on orders over $25.

Every drink can be made with oat, almond or soy milk. Our kitchen handles nuts, gluten, dairy and eggs, so we cannot promise an allergen-free space. Menu tags mark vegan and gluten free items, and our team will happily talk you through the ingredients.

Join at the counter or online. Every drink earns a stamp, the tenth cup is free, and you receive a free pastry on your birthday along with early access to new roasts.

Workshops and cupping nights run on selected weekends. Seats are limited, so book one from the Events section above. If your plans change, tell us 48 hours ahead and we will move you to another date.

Tables for up to eight can be reserved online. For larger groups and private bookings, call +1-555-014-2280 and we will set up the room for you.
