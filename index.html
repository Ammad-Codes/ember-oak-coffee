Our Story

A quieter way to drink coffee

Maison Noir began in 2014 with one small roaster, a short menu and a belief that coffee deserves a little ceremony. We buy directly from growers we have met, roast in small batches every week, and serve each cup on a warm vessel with a short note on what you are tasting.

The room is calm, the lights are low and nothing is rushed. Come for a morning espresso, stay for an afternoon pour over, or take the beans home.
